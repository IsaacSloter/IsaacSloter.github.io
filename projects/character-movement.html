The project

What I worked on

I used Blueprints to work on the player’s movement and character setup. This included float and slam actions, an AI Behavior Tree, and attaching equipment with sockets.

My contributions

  • Set up the player’s Input Actions in Blueprints.
  • Added float and slam actions by changing the character’s gravity.
  • Created an AI Behavior Tree for the platformer.
  • Integrated character animations.
  • Used sockets to attach equipment to the character.
My work & asset credits

The third-party assets were provided by my college or purchased for the project. I worked on the gameplay systems and character setup described here.

Blueprint implementation

Float and slam

  1. Float

    To make the character float, I reduced its gravity scale while the action is active. When the action ends, I reset the gravity scale.

  2. Slam

    For slam, I stopped the character’s current movement and increased its gravity scale. When the action ends, I reset the gravity scale.

AI and character setup

I made an AI Behavior Tree for the platformer and worked on the character Blueprints. I also integrated animations and used sockets to attach equipment to the character.
