Building the interaction.

I’m a gameplay and level designer working in Unreal Engine. My projects explore character movement, interactive puzzle mechanics, and the way levels introduce new ideas to players.

I use Blueprints to implement character behavior and connect gameplay objects. My work includes input actions, pressure-plate and door interactions, animated mechanisms, and puzzle-level progression.

Combat mechanics and level design are areas I want to keep developing. In each case study, I explain my contribution and the thinking behind the work.
